# Reusable Lessons

Status: proposed learning artifact

## Lessons

- Handoff packages need executable validation, not only operating prose.
- Deep Interview should run before KIMI critique or PM debate only when goal, scope, constraints, completion criteria, or impact surface is unclear.
- KIMI should be split into one-time collaborator critique and PM-level debate participant to preserve authority boundaries.
- Research harnesses need a broad reference corpus: BD, finance, company, market, free global reports, and operator-facing decision briefs.
- Compound Extraction belongs after operator decision so it can see final artifacts, review findings, and validation evidence.

## Reuse Conditions

Reuse these lessons for future harness-making runs with `friction_level: 2 | 3` or `purpose_variant: bootstrap_handoff | research | implementation | product_decision | release`.
